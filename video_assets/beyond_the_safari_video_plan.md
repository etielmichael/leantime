## 3. Condensed Narration Script (162 seconds)
```
When the world imagines Kenya, it sees safaris, savannahs, and record-breaking runners. But just beyond the postcard is a nation building the future.

This is "Beyond the Safari"—ten Kenyan innovations shaking the world.

M-Pesa turned basic phones into banks, igniting a financial revolution studied from Harvard to Mumbai.

Ushahidi crowdsources truth, helping responders map disasters from Haiti to the Philippines.

M-Kopa gives families affordable solar power paid daily through mobile money, lighting millions of homes.

BRCK delivers rugged internet routers built for dust, heat, and power cuts, connecting the last mile.

Kenya’s conservationists deploy drones, GPS collars, and AI listening posts to protect endangered wildlife.

Taifa-1, the country’s first homegrown satellite, tracks crops, climate, and a rising space industry.

Sand dams turn dry riverbeds into hidden reservoirs, a model now copied across the globe.

M-Tiba’s mobile health wallet helps citizens save, pay, and receive aid at trusted clinics.

In the Rift Valley, geothermal steam powers the grid as Kenyan engineers train the world.

And in Nairobi’s Silicon Savannah, coders, creators, and dreamers are building Africa’s next tech giant.

Beyond the safari lies a nation of innovators. This is Kenya—and the future is just getting started.
```

## 5. Motion & Edit Notes
- **Transitions:** Cross-dissolves + whip-pans between innovations, glitch cut for tech beats.
- **Text Animations:** Bold sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat ExtraBold). Animate via scale-up from 95% to 100% with 5% letter spacing.
- **Lower Thirds:** Minimal white text over semi-transparent black bar (65% opacity).
- **B-roll Speed Ramping:** Use slight ramp-up (110%) during montage moments for energy.

## 6. Audio & Mix Plan
- **Music:** Cinematic track with evolving layers. Suggestions: Artlist “The Uprising” type cues.
- **SFX:** Soft whooshes on title reveals, subtle drone hum on BRCK + Satellite scenes, nature ambience under wildlife section.
- **VO Processing:** Light compression, -3 dB peak, gentle plate reverb, HPF at 90 Hz.
